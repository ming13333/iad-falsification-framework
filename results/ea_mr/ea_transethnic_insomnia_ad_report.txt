=== Trans-ethnic Insomnia -> AD MR (EUR instruments -> EAS outcome) ===

Exposure: Insomnia ukb-b-3957 (UK Biobank, EUR, n=462,341); 40 LD-independent instruments at r2<0.001, 10 Mb (current 1000G EUR reference; primary EUR analysis reported n=54 under the same thresholds with the July 2026 reference)
Outcome:  GARD/NIAGADS NG00182 East Asian AD (n=6,518, cases=2,919, controls=3,599, lifted hg38->hg19)
Harmonized: 30/40 instruments matched positionally; strand-aligned; 30 retained

--- EUR comparison ---
EUR clumped (54 IVs, loose): beta = -0.130, p = 0.52  [manuscript Level 2]
EAS point estimate: beta = -0.8355, p = 0.2766
Direction: consistent with EUR (negative) — EUR 95% CI [-0.53, 0.27] vs EAS 95% CI [-2.340, +0.670]

--- Power (directional consistency only) ---
R2 instruments (EUR): 0.0028
Power to detect EAS point estimate at alpha=0.05 (2-sided): 0.193
Power to detect EUR clumped beta=-0.13: 0.053

--- Caveats ---
1. EAS outcome small (cases=2,919) — wide CIs; directional-consistency check only, NOT an independent replication
2. EUR instruments transferred trans-ethnically; EUR LD used for clumping (no EAS LD panel available)
3. Instrument count (n=30) differs from primary EUR analysis (n=54) due to LD reference panel update at the public LD server
4. Steiger/coloc/LDSC not possible without EAS LD reference
