# Supplementary Table S6. Exploratory height-to-AD analysis

Height instruments (382 SNPs, F = 78.4) were tested against AD (Kunkle 2019, ieu-b-2) to evaluate whether a truly protective anthropometric trait would be flagged as directional pleiotropy. The IVW estimate was significant (β = −0.107, P = 0.002) but sensitivity methods diverged (MR-Egger β = −0.071, P = 0.44; weighted median β = −0.089, P = 0.063) with substantial heterogeneity (Cochran Q = 431, P = 5 × 10⁻⁵). Height → AD is interpreted as driven by directional pleiotropy and is not used as a formal negative control in the validation cohort.
