# Supplementary Table S10. Leave-one-gate-out ablation of the twelve-case validation cohort

Each of the six gates was removed in turn and the remaining five gates were rescaled to the 0–12 axis (remaining score × 12/10) before the grade thresholds (A ≥ 10, B ≥ 7, C ≥ 4) were re-applied. The procedure is purely arithmetic: it asks which single gate carries each verdict, and it requires no additional data or simulation.

| Gate removed | Grade changes (of 12) | Cases changing grade |
|---|---|---|
| G1 association | 7 | LDL→CAD, education→AD, BMI→T2D (A→B); insomnia→AD, BMI→stroke, HDL→CAD, depression→AD (C→D) |
| G2 robustness | 3 | LDL→CAD, education→AD, BMI→T2D (A→B) |
| G3 architecture | 4 | LDL→CAD, education→AD, BMI→T2D (A→B); insomnia→AD (C→D) |
| G4 direct effect | 4 | LDL→CAD, education→AD, BMI→T2D (A→B); insomnia→AD (C→D) |
| G5 mediation | 3 | LDL→CAD, education→AD, BMI→T2D (A→B) |
| G6 external validation | 0 | none |

**Reading.** No single gate determines the verdict: even the most consequential gate (G1) changes 7 of 12 grades, and G2–G5 change three to four each. G6 carries no weight in any case, because under the revised criterion G6 scores only where an independent-ancestry replication at adequate power is both direction-consistent and nominally significant, and only education→T2D qualifies; that case sits at 7 points after rescaling in every configuration where G6 is removed, so the grade is unchanged. Removing G1 has the largest effect because G1 also drives the cascade that sets a case to zero; the four Grade C cases lose G1 and, rescored on the remaining five gates, fall into Grade D.

The three Grade A cases all sit exactly at the lower A boundary of 10 points, so removing any non-G6 gate drops each of them to 9.6 on the rescaled axis and hence to Grade B. This is a property of the cohort (three cases tied at the threshold), not of the gates: it means the ablation can show how much each gate contributes, but cannot show which gate is uniquely decisive for the strongest cases.

The full 72-row table (12 cases × 6 gates) is provided in the accompanying CSV (`S10_gate_ablation.csv`), generated by `code/build_s8_v3.py` and exported by `code/make_s10_ablation.py`.
