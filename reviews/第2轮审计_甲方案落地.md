### 1.2 R1#3 为何在现有 DGP 下做不出差异（回传最大漏判）

R1#3 要求三路同场比较：①正确 LD 校正的 IVW；②含指定稳健性分析的常规 MR；③完整六门。

**但模拟 DGP 里 `bx = rng.normal(0, 0.12, n_iv)` 各 SNP 独立抽样，`block` 仅作聚类索引** —— 没有任何 LD 相关结构。因此在现有模拟中：

- 臂①「LD 校正 IVW」≡ 臂②的 IVW 部分（无相关可校正）；
- 框架立论的核心机制（相关工具 → 虚假精度 → 需要 LD 校正）在模拟里**不可能出现**；
- 于是三路对照的结果必然是「三条线重合」，反而会坐实「增量价值不足」。

⇒ **不能按回传所说"增加三路同场比较"直接做**。三条可执行替代（按性价比排序）：

| 替代方案 | 内容 | 可行性 | 代价 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A（推荐）** | 用**实证数据**做三路对照：2,462 未 clump IVW（β=+0.10, P=0.0009）／54 LD-independent clumped（β=−0.130, P=0.52）／40 更新面板／全六门 → 直接展示「哪一步改写了结论」 | ✅ 全部数字已在手 | 文字+1 张表，0 重跑 |
| B | 模拟内做**两臂**：常规 MR（IVW + Egger + 加权中位数 + Cochran Q）vs 完整六门 | 🟡 需给 DGP 代码加 3 个估计量并重跑 | 半天量级 |
| C | 给 DGP 注入 LD 相关工具（AR(1) 或块状相关），再跑真正的三路对照 | 🟡 等同新建一套模拟 | 数天，且要重写 Results 的 43,200 结论 |

**建议：先做 A 回应本轮，同时在 Limitations 明写"模拟中未建模工具间 LD 相关，因此本基准不评估 LD 校正本身的增益"** —— 这句话既诚实、又预先掐掉下一轮被追问的可能。

### 2.2 R1#4：FDR 随真值比例 π 的变化（回应"混合比例如何影响 FDR"）

| 多效性 | β | π=0.10 | π=0.33 | π=0.50 | π=0.80 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| None | 0.08 / 0.15 | 0.000 | 0.000 | 0.000 | 0.000 |
| Balanced | 0.08 | 0.024 | 0.099 | 0.183 | 0.472 |
| Balanced | 0.15 | 0.015 | 0.064 | 0.122 | 0.358 |
| Directional | 0.08 | 0.037 | **0.147** | **0.259** | 0.583 |

- π=0.33（= 本网格自身的 null 占比 1/3）时，directional 档 FDR=0.147，落在稿件报告的 0.081–0.183 区间内 → **现有数字可复现**；

- ⇒ 建议主动写进 Limitations：*"the 0.20 FDR threshold is calibrated to a design in which two-thirds of tested claims carry a true effect; under a balanced design (π = 0.5) FDR reached 0.26 in the directional-pleiotropy regime at β = 0.08."* 这是加分项而非减分项。

## 四、Table 1 草案（可替换 L224–L235）

> 说明：占位符 `⟨G4/G5/G6 partial⟩` 处需作者定义判据（见 §3.2）；G1 已按 §3.3 设为二元。

```markdown
**Table 1.** Gate decision matrix for the six-gate falsification framework. Each gate returns one of four
outcomes: pass (2 points), partial (1 point), fail (0 points), or unavailable/underpowered (0 points,
reported explicitly and never counted as support). Scores are summed over the six gates (range 0-12) to
yield an ordinal falsification index. See "The six gates" for operational definitions.

| Gate | Failure mode | Method | Pass (2) | Partial (1) | Fail (0) |
|------|--------------|--------|----------|-------------|----------|
| G1 | Association / screening | Unclumped IVW (liberal screen) | Nominally significant in the claimed direction (P < 0.05) | - (binary gate) | Not significant, or opposite direction |
| G2 | Robustness to LD / overlap / pleiotropy | LD clumping (r2 < 0.001, 10,000 kb, 1000G EUR) + MRlap + CAUSE | Clumped IVW significant in the claimed direction; no directional Egger intercept and no excess heterogeneity (both P >= 0.05) | Clumped IVW significant in the claimed direction but at least one robustness diagnostic marginal or failing | Clumped IVW null, reversed, or CAUSE classified as pleiotropy/sharing |
| G3 | Genetic architecture | LDSC rg + coloc (PP.H4 >= 0.80) | rg consistent with the claim, or null without locus-specific sharing | Independent rg (null or different direction) but locus-specific sharing present | Architectural evidence contradicting the claim |
| G4 | Direct effect | MVMR vs. education, depression, cardiometabolic traits | Significant direct effect in the claimed direction with conditional F > 10 | ⟨G4 partial: to be defined by authors⟩ | Significant effect in the opposite direction, or conditional F <= 10 |
| G5 | Biological mediation | Two-step MR; BH-FDR + bootstrap CI | FDR-corrected indirect effect consistent with the claim | ⟨G5 partial: to be defined by authors⟩ | No FDR-corrected indirect effect along the tested panel |
| G6 | External validation | Cross-ancestry replication in an independent cohort | Directional consistency in the claimed direction (power >= 80%) | ⟨G6 partial: to be defined by authors⟩ | Directional inconsistency, or underpowered and therefore unscored |

*Scores are additive and define an ordinal falsification index (Grade A 10-12, B 7-9, C 4-6, D 0-3);
the score is not a probability or a likelihood, and grades are ordered categories rather than
equal-interval measurements. **Ceiling rule:** a failed robustness gate (G2 = 0) caps the reported
verdict at "not supported" irrespective of the summed score; in the 43,200-replicate benchmark no
replicate with G2 = 0 exceeded 8 points. G1 is scored binarily. Gates reported as unavailable or
underpowered (achieved power < 80%) assign no points and are presented as directional evidence only.
IVW, inverse-variance weighted; LD, linkage disequilibrium; MRlap, sample-overlap-corrected MR;
CAUSE, correlated-pleiotropy MR; LDSC, linkage-disequilibrium score regression; MVMR, multivariable MR;
BH-FDR, Benjamini-Hochberg false discovery rate.*
```

## 五、决定性失败规则：两种落法，代价差 19 个百分点

| 落法 | 具体做法 | 对 Table 2 / S9 / Fig 2 的影响 | 评价 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **① verdict 层**（推荐） | 分数照算（0/1/2 六门），但 G2=0 时最终结论一律写「not supported」 | **不变**（Table 2 描述分数→等级映射） | 逻辑漏洞堵住、数字不动；需在脚注说明两层结构 |
| ② score 层 | G2=0 直接把等级封顶到 C，再重算全部指标 | 🔴 需重算：改级 **4,363/43,200 = 10.1%**；**sens β=0.08 0.526→0.333、β=0.15 0.875→0.765**；spec 不变（1.000/0.796/0.679） | 数字变差 19 个点，且要重画 Fig 2、重出 S9 |

- 12 例中 G2=0 的四例（Insomnia→AD、CRP→CAD、BMI→AD、T2D→AD）已是 C/D，**①与②在这 12 例上结果相同**；
- ⇒ 若选①，可同时给出「②的代价」作为稳健性说明：*"applying the ceiling rule at the score level would reduce sensitivity at β = 0.08 from 0.53 to 0.33 without improving specificity; we therefore apply it at the verdict level."* —— 这句话主动量化代价，比单纯声明更难被攻击。
